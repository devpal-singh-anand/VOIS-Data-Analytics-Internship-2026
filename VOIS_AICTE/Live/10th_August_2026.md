## Program Overview

The **VOIS for Tech** Program on **Data Analytics** is brought by Edunet Foundation, leveraging VOIS and Vodafone Idea Foundation.

VOIS for Tech is an e-learning platform that helps technical students gain foundational skills for market-relevant roles, while earning credentials and receiving guidance from industry experts at no cost.

**Duration:** 4 weeks, from 10 August 2026 to 10 September 2026.

Interns work independently on a project and are assigned a mentor who guides them in identifying a solution to a problem and developing it into a project.

### Benefits

- Access to the VOIS for Tech e-Learning Platform with curated courses on technical skills
- Opportunity to accelerate learning in project-based, collaborative environments
- Masterclasses led by Subject Matter Experts
- Showcase skills by solving real-world challenges
- Certification from AICTE and Edunet Foundation
